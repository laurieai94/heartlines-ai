# hero carousel: modern rooms, same energy

Keep the elders, the gen z fits, and the stiff straight-to-camera portrait humor. Only the interiors change: from retro/dated rooms to how homes actually look today.

## The look

- Contemporary 2026 interiors: clean plaster or painted walls, warm minimal furniture, boucle and linen upholstery, light oak floors, low sofas, arched mirrors, matte black or brushed brass fixtures.
- Modern lived-in details instead of doilies and wood paneling: monstera and fiddle leaf plants, ceramic vases, stacked art books, record players, LED strip glow, flat screen on a low console, open shelving, framed contemporary prints.
- Natural daylight through large windows or sheers, warm and soft. Keep the warm faded film grade the component already applies.

## What stays exactly the same

- Same 10 filenames, slide order, year labels, taglines.
- Same pairings: 3 heterosexual couples, 3 male couples, 3 female couples, plus the solo man holding his framed photo.
- Gen z streetwear, cheesy posed portrait stiffness, distinct faces and outfits, no twinning.
- Very wide framing, subjects in the middle third, generous headroom so the nav bar never clips a head.
