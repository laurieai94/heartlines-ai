# hero carousel: rooms that look like 2025

Only the interiors change. Everyone stays 75+, in gen z fits, posed straight to camera with the same cheesy warmth.

## The look

- Contemporary 2025 homes: warm minimalism, limewash or plaster walls, light oak floors, low boucle and linen seating, arched mirrors, brushed brass or matte black fixtures.
- Modern lived-in layering instead of dated furnishings: large monstera and olive trees, ceramic vases, stacked art books, vinyl setup, curved lamps, gallery walls of contemporary prints, textured throws, chunky rugs.
- Warm natural daylight through big windows or linen sheers. Keep the existing film grade and grain already applied in the component.

## What stays exactly the same

- Same 10 filenames, slide order, year labels, taglines.
- Same pairings: 3 heterosexual couples, 3 male couples, 3 female couples, plus the solo man holding the color portrait of his wife.
- All subjects read 75+. Gen z streetwear, distinct outfits per person, no matching fits.
- Couples posed close and affectionate.
- Very wide framing with generous headroom so no head gets cropped at any viewport.
