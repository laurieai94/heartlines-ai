# hero carousel: no matching outfits, couples act like couples

Two fixes across the set. Right now several slides read as matching-uniform pairs (Atlanta in the same yellow tee and cargos, Honolulu in head-to-toe sage, San Francisco in the same green cargo pants, Miami in near-identical silhouettes), and everyone stands apart like strangers at a DMV.

## 1. Outfits must clearly differ

Within every frame the two people get different colors, different silhouettes, different layers, different headwear. No shared palette, no matching sets, no same-pants-different-jacket. Still all gen z streetwear: baggy jeans, cargos, oversized graphic tees, cropped puffers, hoodies, bucket hats, beanies, chunky sneakers, wire sunglasses, chains. Different hair, heights, and builds too.

## 2. Couples read as in love

Replace the stiff arms-at-sides lineup with warm physical closeness while keeping the posed straight-to-camera portrait humor:

- Arms around waists or shoulders, hands held, leaning into each other, one resting a head on the other's shoulder, a hand on a knee when seated.
- Soft real smiles and eye contact with the camera, affectionate and a little goofy rather than solemn.
- Bodies actually touching, not a gap between them.
- The Detroit loft slide stays solo, holding his framed photo, but softens to a warm expression.

## What stays the same

- Same 10 filenames, slide order, year labels, taglines.
- Same pairings: 3 heterosexual couples, 3 male couples, 3 female couples, plus the solo man.
- Same modern 2026 interiors and regional identities just set for each slide.
- Very wide framing, subjects in the middle third, generous headroom so the nav bar never clips a head, floor visible.
- Warm faded film grade, no orange cast. No component changes.
