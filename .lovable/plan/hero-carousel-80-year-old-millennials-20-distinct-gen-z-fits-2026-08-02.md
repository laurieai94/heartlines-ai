## The vibe

These are people who were 25 in 2025 and are now 80. Their homes look like it. Not "tasteful minimal boomer house" but a home someone who grew up online still lives in:

- Big wall-mounted flat screen or ultrawide monitor, soundbar, LED strip glow behind the panel
- Smart speaker, robot vacuum docked, tablet on a stand, wireless charger, ring light in a corner
- Gaming chair or handheld console on the couch, headphones on a hook, mechanical keyboard
- Sneaker shelf with collectible kicks, vinyl next to a bluetooth turntable, art toys and figures on open shelving
- Still warm and lived in: plants, layered rugs, gallery wall, throws, clutter that reads like a real home
